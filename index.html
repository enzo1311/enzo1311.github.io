✨ What Is codegraph?

codegraph is a small helper that builds an organised map of your code — a "knowledge graph" — and keeps that map up to date automatically whenever your files change.

Modern AI assistants have to read through your project to understand it. That reading takes time, costs tokens (the units of text an AI works with), and often needs several back-and-forth tool calls before it finds the right file. codegraph does that scanning work ahead of time and hands your assistant a clean, pre-built picture instead.

The result is simple: your assistant answers faster, asks fewer questions, and uses far fewer tokens to do the same job. And because everything stays on your machine, nothing about your code leaves your computer.

Will this slow down my computer?

The first scan reads through your project once, which is the busiest moment. Afterwards, updates only touch the files you have just changed, so the work in the background is very small.

What if my project changes a lot?

That is exactly what codegraph is designed for. It watches for changes and refreshes the relevant parts of the map automatically, so you never have to rebuild it by hand.

Do I need a paid account?

No. codegraph runs locally and does not require a subscription or sign-in. Your existing AI assistant setup stays exactly as it is.

What does "fewer tokens" actually mean for me?

Tokens are how AI tools measure the amount of text they process. When your assistant reads a small, well-organised summary instead of whole folders, it uses fewer tokens — which usually means faster answers and lower usage on any plan that counts usage.
